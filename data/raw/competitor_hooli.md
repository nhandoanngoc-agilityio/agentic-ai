# Hooli Insights — Competitor Profile

## Company Overview

Hooli Insights is the business intelligence product of Hooli's cloud
platform. It is sold through the cloud console alongside storage,
warehousing and machine-learning services, and most customers buy it as
part of a broader cloud commitment rather than as a standalone tool.
Hooli reports about 21,000 customers using Insights, ranging from
startups on the free cloud credits program to large enterprises running
their data warehouse on Hooli.

### Leadership and Headcount

The BI unit has roughly 1,100 employees, a mix of product engineers and
field specialists embedded in the cloud sales organisation. Insights is
run as a product line inside the cloud division, and its roadmap follows
the division's priorities: tighter integration with the warehouse,
identity and machine-learning services.

## Product and Pricing

Hooli lists Insights at $25 per seat per month. Customers with an annual
cloud commitment receive a discounted rate of $18 per seat per month,
which in practice is what most enterprise buyers pay. Viewer-only
accounts for people who read dashboards but never build them are
included without a separate charge.

### Strengths

Distribution is the main strength: Insights is one click away for every
Hooli cloud customer, billing runs through the existing cloud account,
and security reviews are already done. Query performance against
Hooli's own warehouse is strong because the two products share an engine
and a metadata catalogue.

### Weaknesses

Outside Hooli's cloud, Insights is weak. Connectors to other clouds and
on-premises databases are limited, and customers with data spread across
providers describe it as a poor fit. Some buyers also worry about
deepening dependence on a single cloud vendor, and the product's
release cadence follows the cloud division rather than BI buyers.

## Recent Moves

Hooli has shipped a natural-language query feature that answers
questions about warehouse tables in plain language and suggests charts.
It is included in the existing seat price. The company has also begun
bundling Insights into new cloud contracts by default, a move
competitors describe as aggressive.
