# Globex Insight — Competitor Profile

## Company Overview

Globex Insight is an enterprise-focused analytics platform that has been
operating since 2009, making it one of the more established players in the
space. The company is privately held, does not disclose revenue, and is
estimated by industry trackers to serve around 400 large accounts, each
paying six figures annually on average. Globex targets Fortune 1000
companies directly and rarely competes on price.

### Leadership and Headcount

Globex is headquartered in Boston with an estimated 900 employees globally,
including a large professional-services organization that implements and
customizes deployments for enterprise clients. Unlike self-serve
competitors, most Globex contracts include a mandatory implementation
package delivered by Globex's own consultants, typically spanning eight to
sixteen weeks.

## Product and Pricing

Globex does not publish list pricing; deals are negotiated individually and
typically bundle platform licensing with the implementation-services
engagement described above. Industry estimates place typical annual
contract value between $150K and $400K depending on data volume, number of
embedded-analytics use cases, and support tier.

### Strengths

Globex's core strength is depth: its semantic layer supports complex,
governed metric definitions shared across the organization, and its
row-level and column-level security model is widely regarded as best in
class for regulated industries such as healthcare and financial services.
Customer references consistently cite white-glove support and a named
technical account manager as reasons for renewal.

### Weaknesses

The mandatory implementation engagement and lack of a self-serve tier make
Globex a poor fit for smaller teams or fast-moving evaluations — sales
cycles commonly run four to nine months. Several churned customers cited
the platform's dashboard-authoring UI as dated relative to newer entrants,
and noted that minor changes to existing reports often require raising a
ticket with the professional-services team rather than being self-service.

## Recent Moves

Globex recently announced a new "Insight Copilot" module that layers a
conversational assistant on top of its existing semantic layer, positioned
explicitly as a governed alternative to more freewheeling natural-language
query tools from newer entrants. Early access is limited to existing
Enterprise-tier customers, and general availability has not been dated.
