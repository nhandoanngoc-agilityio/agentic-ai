# Customer Review Digest

This digest summarises public reviews and buyer interviews about
business intelligence vendors. Ratings are average scores out of five
across reviews published in the period covered; themes are those raised
most often by reviewers.

## Customer Ratings

### Acme

Acme averages 4.3 out of 5. Reviewers praise the connector library and
fast onboarding, often describing a first dashboard within days.
Complaints focus on the separately priced transform add-on and on
row-level security being reserved for the Enterprise tier.

### Initech

Initech averages 4.5 out of 5, the highest in this digest. Small teams
value the low price and the template gallery. Growing customers report
slow refreshes on larger datasets and want stronger governance.

### Umbrella

Umbrella averages 4.1 out of 5. Product teams praise the SDK and the
solutions engineers. The most common complaint is unpredictable cost as
viewer counts grow, followed by a basic internal dashboard tool.

### Hooli

Hooli averages 3.9 out of 5. Reviewers like the convenience of buying
through the cloud console and the speed against Hooli's own warehouse,
but criticise weak connectors to other clouds and a release schedule
that ignores BI requests.

### Globex

Globex averages 3.7 out of 5. Enterprise reviewers praise the depth of
the platform and the implementation partners, while mid-market buyers
describe long deployments and opaque negotiated pricing.

### Vandelay

Vandelay averages 4.4 out of 5 among logistics companies. Reviewers
highlight freight metrics that work on the first day and a vocabulary
that matches their operations. The seat minimum and the high per-seat
price are the most cited drawbacks.

## Common Themes

Across vendors, reviewers reward fast time to a first useful dashboard
and penalise pricing they cannot predict. Governance features matter
more as customers grow, and natural-language query features attract
interest but mixed reviews on accuracy. Support quality is mentioned in
almost every review and strongly affects scores.
