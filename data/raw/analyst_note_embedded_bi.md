# Analyst Note — Embedded Analytics Outlook

Embedded analytics, where software vendors put charts and dashboards
inside their own products, has become one of the most active corners of
the business intelligence market. Buyers in this segment are product
and engineering leaders rather than data teams, and they judge vendors
on developer experience, theming and multi-tenant security more than on
the breadth of dashboard features.

## Segment Outlook

### Umbrella

Umbrella is the reference vendor for white-label embedding. Demand from
vertical software companies remains strong, and its SDK sets the bar for
how much of the experience a customer can restyle. The open question is
pricing: viewer-based fees align cost with value early on but create
friction when a customer's product scales, and we expect more customers
to push for caps or committed-use agreements.

### Hooli

Hooli is approaching embedding from the cloud side. By bundling
analytics components into cloud contracts it lowers the cost of trying
embedded features for teams already on its platform. We see this as a
credible threat to specialist vendors among cloud-native software
companies, less so among companies spread across several providers.

### Globex

Globex is pursuing enterprise embedding, where large companies build
internal portals for partners and suppliers. Its services-heavy model
suits these long projects but leaves it exposed if buyers come to expect
self-service tooling and faster deployments.

## What to Watch

Three trends will shape the segment. First, natural-language query
components are moving from demonstrations into products that customers
can embed, and vendors that make them safe to expose to end users will
gain an edge. Second, buyers increasingly want analytics to respect the
permissions of the host application without duplicate configuration.
Third, procurement teams are scrutinising usage-based pricing and asking
for predictable spend, which favours vendors willing to offer committed
tiers. Vendors that combine a strong developer experience with
predictable pricing are best placed to win in this segment.
