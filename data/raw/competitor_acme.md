# Acme Analytics — Competitor Profile

## Company Overview

Acme Analytics is a mid-market business intelligence vendor founded in 2015
and headquartered in Austin, Texas. The company has raised $68M across three
funding rounds and reports roughly 1,200 paying customers, concentrated in
retail, logistics, and financial services. Acme's core pitch is "BI without
the data team" — a drag-and-drop dashboard builder paired with a managed
ETL layer that connects to common SaaS sources (Salesforce, NetSuite,
Shopify, Stripe) without requiring customers to maintain their own pipelines.

### Leadership and Headcount

Acme employs approximately 340 people as of the most recent count, with
engineering concentrated in Austin and a growing go-to-market team in
Chicago. The CEO, a former product lead at a larger BI incumbent, has
publicly emphasized a "land and expand" motion: land with a single team on
a low-cost starter tier, then expand seat count and data-source connectors
as the customer's usage grows.

## Product and Pricing

Acme prices per seat with a data-volume overage component, starting at $49
per seat per month on the Starter tier (up to 3 connectors, 10M rows/month)
and scaling to a custom Enterprise tier with SSO, row-level security, and a
dedicated customer success manager. There is no perpetual free tier, but a
14-day trial is offered without a credit card requirement.

### Strengths

Acme's managed-connector library is its clearest differentiator — over 180
pre-built integrations, more than most direct competitors, and a
self-service connector-request process that reportedly ships new
integrations within two to four weeks of a customer request. Onboarding
time is frequently cited in reviews as under one week for standard use
cases, well below the industry-reported average of three to six weeks for
comparable BI platforms.

### Weaknesses

Acme's dashboarding layer lacks native support for custom SQL transforms,
pushing power users toward a separate (and separately priced) "Acme
Transform" add-on. Several reviews also flag that row-level security is
Enterprise-only, which limits adoption among mid-market customers in
regulated industries that need it but can't justify the Enterprise price
point.

## Recent Moves

In the last two quarters, Acme announced a partnership with a major cloud
data warehouse vendor to offer a reverse-ETL connector, and shipped an
AI-assisted "ask your data" natural-language query feature currently in
public beta. Pricing for the natural-language feature has not yet been
disclosed, and early reviews describe accuracy as inconsistent on
multi-table joins.
