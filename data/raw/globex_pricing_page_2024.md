# Globex Insight — Pricing (archived)

## Pricing

This is an archived copy of the Globex Insight pricing page. Globex does
not sell its platform through a self-service checkout and does not
publish per-seat rates. Every customer receives a quote prepared by an
account executive after a discovery workshop with the customer's data,
engineering and business teams.

According to the page, annual contract value runs from $120K to $350K
depending on data volume, the number of embedded analytics use cases and
the support tier selected. Implementation services are billed
separately, either by Globex's own professional-services team or by a
certified partner, and are scoped in a statement of work that runs
alongside the licence agreement.

The page describes three support tiers. Standard support covers business
hours and a shared queue. Premium support adds round-the-clock coverage
for critical incidents and a named technical account manager. Strategic
support adds quarterly architecture reviews and early access to new
features through a customer advisory board. Customers can move between
tiers at renewal.

Licensing is described as platform-based rather than seat-based: a
customer licenses environments and data capacity, and internal users and
external viewers of embedded dashboards are not counted individually.
The page argues that this suits enterprises that expose analytics to
large partner and supplier networks, where per-seat pricing would be
unpredictable.

The page also lists what every contract includes: the core platform,
the governance and lineage modules, the embedding toolkit, access to the
documentation portal and training courses, and a sandbox environment for
development and testing. Optional modules for advanced forecasting and
industry data models are quoted separately.

Prospective customers are invited to request a pricing consultation
through a contact form. The page notes that multi-year agreements and
commitments across several business units are eligible for
discounts, but it does not state their size.
