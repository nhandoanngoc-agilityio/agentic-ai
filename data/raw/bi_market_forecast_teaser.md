# BI Market Outlook — Preview Edition

## What the Full Report Covers

This preview introduces the forthcoming edition of our outlook on the
business intelligence market. It describes the scope and approach of the
full report but does not include its estimates. Market size, growth and
segment forecasts are available only in the paid edition, which
subscribers can download from the research portal.

The full report sizes the market for business intelligence platforms,
embedded analytics and data preparation tools, and breaks the totals
down by region, company size and industry. It projects spending over the
coming years under a base case and two alternative scenarios, one
assuming faster adoption of natural-language query tools and one
assuming tighter technology budgets.

Beyond sizing, the report profiles the competitive landscape. It
examines how cloud platform vendors are bundling analytics into broader
contracts, how vertical specialists defend their niches, and how
self-serve vendors are moving up toward larger customers. Each vendor
profile covers positioning, pricing model, customer base and recent
product direction, based on briefings and buyer interviews.

The report also includes a buyer section built from a survey of data and
analytics leaders. It covers how organisations choose platforms, which
capabilities they plan to add, what drives switching, and how they
evaluate usage-based pricing compared with per-seat licensing.

Finally, the report sets out the themes our analysts expect to shape the
market: generative and natural-language interfaces, consolidation among
smaller vendors, the convergence of analytics with data engineering
tools, and rising expectations for governance and cost predictability.

The methodology chapter explains how the estimates are built: vendor
revenue disclosures, channel checks with resellers and implementation
partners, and the buyer survey are combined and reconciled, and every
assumption is listed so readers can adjust the scenarios themselves.

This preview is provided for planning purposes only. Readers who need
figures for budgeting, investment or competitive decisions should rely
on the full edition rather than on this summary, which intentionally
omits every quantitative estimate.
