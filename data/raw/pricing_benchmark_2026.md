# BI Pricing Benchmark — 2026

## Per-Seat Pricing by Vendor

This benchmark compares the current list prices of business intelligence
vendors that sell to mid-market buyers. Prices are per month unless
stated otherwise and exclude negotiated discounts, implementation
services and add-ons sold separately.

### Acme

Acme now lists its Starter tier at $55 per seat per month, still with up
to 3 connectors and 10M rows per month included. The custom Enterprise
tier with single sign-on, row-level security and a dedicated customer
success manager is unchanged, as is the 14-day trial without a credit
card.

### Initech

Initech's Team plan is $15 per seat per month and its Business plan is
$29 per seat per month. A 30-day free trial is available on both plans,
and annual billing uses the same rates.

### Umbrella

Umbrella does not price by seat. It charges a platform fee of $2,000 per
month plus $0.40 per monthly active viewer of the embedded analytics,
which makes its cost depend on the success of the customer's own
product rather than on headcount.

### Hooli

Hooli lists Insights at $25 per seat per month. With an annual cloud
commitment the rate falls to $18 per seat per month, the price most
enterprise buyers actually pay.

### Vandelay

Vandelay charges $70 per seat per month with a minimum of 20 seats, the
highest per-seat price in this benchmark, reflecting its vertical focus
on logistics and the prebuilt freight data model included in every
contract.

### Globex

Globex still publishes no list price. Deals are negotiated individually
and usually bundle platform licensing with implementation services, so
it could not be placed on the per-seat comparison.

## Methodology

List prices were collected from vendor websites and pricing pages and
checked against buyer surveys and procurement records shared by
participating companies. Where a vendor offers several tiers, the entry
tier aimed at business teams is shown first. Promotional prices, partner
discounts and usage overages are excluded, and vendors were invited to
correct any figure before publication.
